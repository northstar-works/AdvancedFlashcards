HOW KENPO CARDS REACH THE APP NOW
- Full sync pull (GET /api/sync/user_cards) returns per-user cards PLUS all
  deck-scoped cards from accessible decks (kenpo included), tagged
  serverManaged=true. They land in the existing user-cards store with
  deckId="kenpo", so activeCardsFlow's kenpo filter picks them up unchanged.
- Pushback safety lives server-side: non-owner copies of serverManaged cards
  are skipped; owner/admin edits are routed into the deck's storage.
- Fresh install before first login shows an empty Kenpo placeholder deck; this
  is intended (no decks ship with the app anymore).
